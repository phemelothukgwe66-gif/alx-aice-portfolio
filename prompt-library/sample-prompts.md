## 📌 Prompt 1: Strategic Content Ideation & Campaign Narrative
* **Objective:** Guide an LLM to generate an authentic, mission-driven campaign narrative without sounding generic or relying on standard templates.
* **Target Audience:** Young creators, changemakers, and community leaders.

### The Prompt Structure (Role, Task, Context, Constraints)
> **Role:** Act as an expert brand copywriter and AI ethics consultant.
> **Context:** We are launching a community initiative called the *Phemelo Thukgwe Foundation* to uplift young creators and artists.
> **Task:** Write a short, powerful narrative (150-200 words) highlighting resilience, creativity, and opportunity.
> **Constraints:** 
> - Avoid clichés and overly corporate jargon.
> - Ensure tone is inspiring, grounded, and human.
> - Protect privacy: use no real personal data or identifiable faces.

### Result / Output Highlight
*Successfully generated a balanced narrative that kept human authorship central, ensuring the messaging aligned with community values while utilizing AI for fluid structuring and tone refinement.*

## 📌 Prompt 2: AI Safety & Ethics Checklist Framework
* **Objective:** Systematically audit an AI-assisted workflow for data privacy, bias, and operational safety before deployment.

### The Prompt Structure
> **Role:** Act as a Chief AI Ethics Officer.
> **Task:** Evaluate the following workflow for potential algorithmic bias, data privacy risks, and over-reliance on automated outputs.
> **Input:** [Insert workflow description or draft email/report here]
> **Checklist Requirements:** Verify tool approval status, check for sensitive personal data entry, and confirm human-in-the-loop review steps.

### Result / Output Highlight
*Created a robust evaluation process ensuring all AI-generated content is vetted, fact-checked, and compliant with responsible data protection standards.*
